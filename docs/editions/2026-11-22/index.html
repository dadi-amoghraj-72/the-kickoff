Arsenal

  • Premier League · Newcastle United 2–2 Arsenal

Newcastle United and Arsenal drew 2–2. Eberechi Eze put Arsenal ahead in the 33rd minute, set up by Kai Havertz.

Harvey Barnes levelled in the 49th minute from a William Osula assist, and Malick Thiaw made it 2–1 to Newcastle United in the 62nd minute, with Joe Willock providing the assist.

Noni Madueke equalised for Arsenal in the 78th minute, assisted by Martin Ødegaard.

Arsenal are first with 28 points from 11 games, and their last five read 3W 2D 0L, with an unbeaten run of 10. Newcastle United are 12th on 13 points.

Manchester City and Fulham drew 1–1. Rodrigo Muniz put Fulham ahead in the 16th minute, and Elliot Anderson levelled in the 57th.

City are third on 22 points after 11 games, while Fulham are 15th on 11 points.

Leeds United won 4–1 at Chelsea. Emmanuel Emegha scored first for Chelsea in the 25th minute, but Noah Okafor scored twice for Leeds, Dominic Calvert-Lewin added a penalty, and Brenden Aaronson scored in stoppage time.

Leeds climbed from ninth to sixth on 17 points; Chelsea dropped from fifth to eighth on 16.

Tottenham Hotspur beat Ipswich Town 2–0, with goals from Luca Williams-Barnett in the 15th minute and Dominic Solanke in the 51st.

Spurs rose from 11th to ninth on 15 points. Ipswich dropped from 14th to 16th on 11 points.

AFC Bournemouth beat Nottingham Forest 2–0. Evanilson scored in the 19th minute and Justin Kluivert added a second in first-half stoppage time.

Bournemouth climbed from seventh to fourth on 18 points, while Forest dropped from 16th to 17th on 9.

Aston Villa and Sunderland drew 0–0. Villa slipped from 10th to 11th on 14 points, while Sunderland moved up from 19th to 18th on 9 points.

Coventry City beat Crystal Palace 3–2. Taiwo Awoniyi and Tatsuhiro Sakamoto scored for Coventry, Yéremy Pino and Jørgen Strand Larsen replied for Palace, and Dwight McNeil's own goal in the 85th minute decided it.

Coventry climbed from 17th to 13th on 12 points; Palace are 20th on 7.
